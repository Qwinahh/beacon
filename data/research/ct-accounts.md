Sources: live mirror scrapes (sotwe.com, twstalker.com, fetched 2026-06-12), ThreadReaderApp archives, web search results quoting tweets, and press coverage. Direct x.com fetches were not possible; see Caveats.

## @0xSisyphus — "Sisyphus"

**Profile (verified live 2026-06-12 via sotwe.com):** 154K followers, 41.9K posts, joined March 2021. Bio: "flight enthusiast". Pinned tweet: "One must imagine Sisyphus happy." (~2021).

**Topics**
- Market commentary and "smart money" takes (Hyperliquid/$HYPE was a signature call), crypto-industry meta-commentary, scam/grift callouts (Protos profiled him as a crypto "scam cop": https://protos.com/from-60m-failure-to-crypto-scam-cop-the-reinvention-of-0xsisyphus/)
- As of June 2026 his visible timeline is heavily retweets spanning macro (zerohedge, FirstSquawk), AI (SemiAnalysis, citrini), TradFi, and crypto news — he functions partly as a curator account now (observed directly on mirror, 2026-06-12).

**Formats that get engagement**
- One-liner spicy takes and aphorisms; no threads to speak of (ThreadReader has essentially nothing for him).
- Running bits/memes — e.g. ending unrelated tweets with the word "Hyperliquid" (the bit spread; e.g. @haralabob ended a June 2026 macro tweet "Anyways Hyperliquid." — seen on mirror 2026-06-12).

**Frequency (estimate, inferred):** ~41.9K posts since Mar 2021 ≈ 20+ posts/day average including retweets/replies. Heavy retweeter; original takes a few per day.

**Images:** Rare in original posts; text-first. Retweets carry others' charts/screenshots. (Inferred from observed timeline.)

**Voice**
- Blunt, deadpan, contrarian, comfortable being abrasive. Normal sentence case, minimal punctuation ceremony, no emojis, no hashtags.

## Cross-account patterns (what high-engagement formats share)

1. **Two winning archetypes, don't mix registers mid-post:** (a) short, blunt, personality-driven one-liners (Sisyphus, Gainzy, Pentoshi's singles); (b) structured value threads with a hook → stakes → numbered payoff (DefiEdge, Route2FI, Ignas). The thread-writers still post daily short takes between threads.
2. **The hook formula for threads:** big claim or stat + urgency/stakes + explicit promise of a list ("Here's your guide to X (and the top protocols):"). Curiosity gap in tweet 1; payoff distributed; bookmark CTA mid-thread.
3. **Numbers in the first line** outperform adjectives: "671k $ETH = $3.2B", "1.2B to 2.2B in 7 days", "$16 Trillion by 2030", "27% APY".
4. **One line per sentence, white space everywhere.** All six write in short lines with hard breaks; none write paragraphs (Ignas comes closest).
5. **$CASHTAGS standard, hashtags essentially never** (none of the six use hashtags).
6. **Skin in the game + transparency builds trust:** disclosing positions, losses, and mistakes (Pentoshi's round-trip loss, Route2FI's UST mea culpa, Ignas's farming) reliably drives engagement and credibility.
7. **Signature bits compound:** a repeated meme/sign-off (Hyperliquid suffix, "Here's your Edge 🗡️", penguin emoji, "1/" data hooks) makes posts instantly attributable.
8. **Images:** analysts attach charts/branded covers to nearly everything; shitposters use memes/PnL screenshots; pure-text works only for the aphorism archetype.
9. **Self-aware humor about CT itself** is an evergreen engagement well (Gainzy's whole act, Pentoshi's parody threads, DefiEdge's Yap-farming thread, Ignas's "threaaddiing").
10. **Long-form X articles** were called out (DefiEdge, Jan 2025) as algorithm-favored — worth testing.
