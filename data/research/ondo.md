### Competitive position vs BUIDL / Franklin / Superstate

- In tokenized Treasuries, BlackRock BUIDL leads single-fund AUM ($2.9B+, ~40% share); Ondo's OUSG (~$680M) + USDY (~$740M) together are roughly comparable to BUIDL's nearest rivals and well ahead of Franklin BENJI (~$700–750M as a single fund). Superstate USTB is positioned as a qualified-purchaser private fund; no current AUM figure was found in searches. Source: https://stablecoininsider.org/top-10-tokenized-treasury-funds-in-2026-buidl-benji-and-the-highest-yielding-on-chain-options/ ; https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15210582-top-tokenized-treasury-funds-2026-buidl-ousg-usdy-benji-compared
- Ondo's differentiation: it is not just a fund issuer — it owns >70% of the tokenized *equities* market (Ondo GM), runs multi-chain distribution (12+ chains per DeFiLlama), and is building its own L1 (Ondo Chain). BUIDL/BENJI/USTB do not compete in tokenized stocks.
