## Token status

- **No POLY token has launched as of 2026-06-11.** No price, no market cap, no confirmed airdrop date. (Source: https://coincodex.com/article/67098/polymarket-airdrop/ and https://www.kucoin.com/blog/polymarket-airdrop-2026-token-launch-predictions)
- Token + airdrop were **confirmed as planned** by CMO Matthew Modabber (Degenz Live podcast, Oct 2025): "there will be a coin and there will be an airdrop," expected ticker POLY. (https://99bitcoins.com/news/bitcoin-btc/polymarket-confirms-poly-token-launch-and-airdrop-plans/)
- Community estimates put the token event in **H2 2026**; speculated 5–10% of supply to airdrop participants. These are unofficial. (https://coincodex.com/article/67098/polymarket-airdrop/)
- Separately, Polymarket shifted users to **"Polymarket USD"** (a new settlement token/stablecoin) this spring — the company says the transition contributed to the May volume dip. Not the POLY token. (CNBC, 2026-06-10: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/10/polymarkets-volume-falls-again-in-may.html)

### US regulatory status (context)
- Polymarket received CFTC approval of an amended order of designation enabling intermediated US market access (late 2025) and returned to US users in early January 2026 after a ~3-year hiatus. (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/polymarket-receives-cftc-approval-of-amended-order-of-designation-enabling-intermediated-us-market-access-302625833.html ; https://reason.com/2026/01/04/the-return-of-polymarket/)
- The June 2026 CFTC NPRM will define which event contracts are permitted federally — a key catalyst/risk for both Polymarket and Kalshi.

### Kalshi competition
- Kalshi out-traded Polymarket in May 2026: >$17.9B vs ~$7.1B, and Kalshi keeps growing while Polymarket's volume declined two straight months. (CNBC, 2026-06-10)
- Sports mix: ~80% of Kalshi volume vs ~39% of Polymarket volume since July 2024. (https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/05/27/trading-volume-on-prediction-markets-has-soared-in-recent-months/)
- A Polymarket market priced Polymarket at ~47% vs Kalshi ~34% to finish 2026 as volume leader (as of 2026-01-23); newcomers (Robinhood, "Opinion") taking share. (https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/predictstreet-2026-1-23-the-great-prediction-war-of-2026-polymarket-and-kalshi-battle-for-dominance-as-information-finance-goes-mainstream)
