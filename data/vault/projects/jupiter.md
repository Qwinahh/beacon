# Jupiter

## Thesis
Jupiter is the best-run product in Solana DeFi and the most instructive
token failure in crypto. The aggregator did $17.06B in 30d volume
(2026-06-11, DeFiLlama) and held 93.6% of Solana aggregator-routed flow
(late Dec 2025) — yet JUP sits at ~$0.15, −92% from ATH, after the team
spent $70M+ on buybacks in 2025 that founder Siong publicly admitted
"obviously didn't move" price. The superapp is real: perps+JLP, Lend,
JupUSD (90% backed by BlackRock BUIDL, built with Ethena), Mobile V3,
and now Forecast prediction markets (Jun 5). The lesson CT keeps
relearning through JUP: emissions beat buybacks, every time.

## Stance
Using the products, holding a small staked JUP position for ASR — the Q1
2026 claim window runs to 2026-07-08, and governance-stake yield on an
asset I'd watch anyway is acceptable carry. Not sizing up while ~53M
JUP/month unlocks through June 2026. The actually interesting trade
setup: unlock schedule ends mid-2026 — if emissions stop and the
superapp keeps compounding, the supply/demand math flips for the first
time in the token's life.

## Risks
- Buyback status is in flux (halt proposed Jan 2026; current state unverified — do not post "buybacks are dead" as fact)
- JupUSD concentration: converting ~$750M of JLP stables to in-house stablecoin stacks ecosystem risk on one issuer
- Superapp sprawl — perps, lend, stable, mobile, predictions — is execution risk wearing ambition's clothes
- 93.6% share is a Dec 2025 reading; current share unverified
