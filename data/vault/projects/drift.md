# Drift

## Thesis
Drift is not a Hyperliquid competitor right now — it's a recovery story.
Hacked for $295.4M on 2026-04-01 (Mandiant attributes it to UNC6862, a DPRK
state group, via Solana durable nonces). Perps have been suspended ever
since. The Tether-led rescue (~$148M, USDC swapped out for USDT settlement)
keeps it alive, but the relaunch already slipped its May–June window. The
real thesis: can a nine-figure exploit be survived by a perps DEX at all?
Nobody has done it. If Drift pulls it off, the recovery-token mechanism
becomes the template for every future DeFi hack.

## Stance
No position. Not farming. Watching the relaunch as an event, not a trade.
DRIFT at $0.064 (2026-06-11, CoinGecko) is a $37M mcap option on a
successful reboot — but price touching the $0.0533 ATL while the team posts
recovery updates tells you the market isn't buying it yet. The Upbit
delisting (2026-06-01) makes the float even uglier. If perps resume and the
recovery pool actually scales toward the promised $151M, reassess.

## Risks
- Relaunch slips again — every week of delay bleeds remaining users to Hyperliquid and Jupiter Perps
- Recovery pool starts at ~$3.8M against $295.4M in losses; the path to full coverage runs through revenue that doesn't exist while trading is suspended
- USDT settlement ties the protocol's fate to Tether's continued sponsorship
- Pre-hack OI (~$700M) was ~7% of Hyperliquid's current $10.2B — even full recovery returns it to a distant second tier
