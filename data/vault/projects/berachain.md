# Berachain

## Thesis
Berachain is the cleanest natural experiment in crypto: can clever
tokenomics manufacture durable liquidity? Answer came back no. TVL went
$3.31B (Mar 2025) → $54.7M (Jun 2026), −98.3%, and BERA printed a fresh
all-time low of $0.227 on 2026-06-10. Now the team is killing its own
invention: "PoL Next" (mainnet 2026-06-23) deprecates BGT entirely —
no governance, no boost, residual BGT auto-converts to WBERA. Fixed
WBERA emissions, sWBERA as the single value sink, ERAs (multi-month
emission deals for revenue-generating apps). The tritoken thesis is
formally dead; what launches June 23 is a different chain wearing the
same fur.

## Stance
Not farming, no position. BGT-loop farming is over by design. The honest
frame for June 23: a ~$66-69M mcap chain with working infrastructure,
audited new tokenomics, and almost zero remaining attention — that's
either a graveyard or an asymmetric reset, and sWBERA yield data
post-activation decides which. Kodiak ($46.5M) and Infrared ($30.2M)
are the only protocols that matter; they ARE the chain TVL.

## Risks
- PoL Next could trigger final capitulation — BGT holders forced to migrate may just exit
- ERAs are emissions-for-revenue deals; if the revenue apps don't come, it's emissions-for-nothing with extra steps
- HONEY status is a black box — no verifiable 2026 supply/peg data (do not quote HONEY numbers)
- Mcap data is messy (CoinPaprika uses stale supply); use ~$66-69M, not $27M
