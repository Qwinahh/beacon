# Ondo Finance

## Thesis
Everyone files Ondo under "tokenized T-bills" and that's last year's
company. The Treasuries business (OUSG ~$680M, USDY ~$740M at 4.65%) is
the stable base, but the growth engine is Ondo Global Markets: >$1B TVL
in tokenized equities within 8 months of launch, >70% of the tokenized-
equity issuer market, $18B cumulative volume, 260+ US stocks/ETFs across
Solana/Ethereum/BNB (2026-05-14), plus EU approval covering 30 countries.
Ondo Perps — 20x leverage on tokenized stocks, announced for June 9 —
completes the stack: issue the asset, run the market, then sell the
leverage. That's an exchange business wearing an RWA costume.

## Stance
No ONDO position at $1.7B mcap — the institutional story is real but the
token's claim on Global Markets revenue is as unproven as every other
"strong product" token in this vault. Respect the execution: while BUIDL
($2.9B, ~40% of tokenized Treasuries) wins the fund race, Ondo sidestepped
into a market BlackRock doesn't contest. Watching two things: whether
Ondo Perps actually shipped on June 9 (unconfirmed), and Ondo Chain
mainnet (still not verifiably live as of 2026-06-11 despite "early-to-mid
2026" guidance).

## Risks
- Stack concentration: issuer + venue + leverage on the same assets is the kind of vertical integration regulators dismantle first
- Heavy reliance on AI-aggregator-sourced news (Kinexys/Mastercard/Ripple pilot etc.) — several headline partnerships are weakly sourced
- RWA-sector beta: ONDO +17% on red days cuts both ways when the rotation reverses
- Ondo Chain slipping past guidance would dent the institutional-rails narrative
