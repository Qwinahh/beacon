# Ethena

## Thesis
The Ethena everyone argues about no longer exists. USDe is barely a basis
trade anymore: live transparency API (2026-06-11) shows 97.4% of backing
in "Liquid Cash" (stables/T-bill-like/lending) and only ~2.5% in
delta-hedged crypto. The Oct 10, 2025 crash did that — supply collapsed
from $14.6B to $4.48B (−69%) and the team rebuilt the product as a
regulated synthetic-dollar/RWA platform: AAA CLOs via Janus Henderson
(capped ~$310M), institutional lending via Anchorage/Maple, a Coinbase
savings product for 100M users. The new thesis is distribution, not
funding capture.

## Stance
No sUSDe position. 3.5% APY (2026-06-10, ethena.fi API) over T-bills does
not pay for smart-contract + custodial + exchange risk — the lifetime
average was 11.2%, the current print is the worst of both worlds. ENA at
$0.08 (−94% from ATH, $744M mcap) is a different question: two TradFi
stakes in one week (Coinbase Ventures Jun 2, Janus Henderson Jun 9) is
real validation. Still not buying — token accrual remains unproven — but
the bear case got weaker in June.

## Risks
- Reserve fund $62M against $4.48B supply (~1.4%) — thin if anything breaks
- The CLO/credit pivot ADDS credit and duration risk the pure basis design never had; bear case shifted, didn't shrink
- If funding turns strongly positive again, scaling the hedge book back up means re-acquiring all the old CEX counterparty risk at size
- Regulatory: yield-bearing dollar tokens are the SEC's most obvious target
