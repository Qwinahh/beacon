# Aave

## Thesis
Still the lending king, but the crown costs more every quarter. $12.22B
TVL (2026-06-11, DeFiLlama) is ~34% of all DeFi lending and ~1.8x Morpho —
yet TVL has roughly halved from the reported ~$30B Nov 2025 peak, borrow
fees are down ~25% from peak, and the DAO just cut the buyback budget
from $50M to $30M/yr. V4 shipped on mainnet 2026-03-30 (hub-and-spoke,
customizable spokes) but has only $114M TVL — migration is a 2027 story.
Aave's structural advantage is still being THE venue: when a whale wants
$142M of leverage to buy ETH (Jun 6), it happens on Aave.

## Stance
Default lending venue for size, nothing more. AAVE at $64.57 / $979M mcap
with real revenue and mechanical buybacks is one of the few majors with a
P/E argument — but I'm not long while revenue compresses and governance
burns credibility in public (the "Aave Will Win" fight, ACI challenging
vote results). GHO at $598M supply has plateaued; peg is fine ($0.9987),
growth isn't. Watch V4 spoke adoption as the only number that changes
the story.

## Risks
- Revenue decline is structural (rate compression), not cyclical — buyback cut acknowledges it
- KelpDAO rsETH episode put hundreds of millions in bad debt on the book via a bridge exploit; listing standards were overhauled after, not before
- Governance drama (Aave Labs vs ACI) is a slow leak on the institutional pitch
- V4 migration risk: hub-and-spoke is a rewrite, and rewrites ship bugs
