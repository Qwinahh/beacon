# Morpho

## Thesis
Morpho is winning the architecture argument with Aave even while losing
the TVL one. Isolated markets (one collateral, one loan asset, one oracle,
one LLTV) + the curator model (Gauntlet, Steakhouse) unbundle risk from
the protocol — bad debt in one market can't contaminate the rest, which
is exactly the failure mode that hit Aave in the KelpDAO rsETH episode.
The real business is B2B: Coinbase runs its loan book on Morpho (>$2.17B
US originations by 2026-04-14, now UK + SOL collateral). $175M from
Paradigm/a16z/Ribbit at ~$2B (2026-06-09) confirms the "open credit
network" pitch is landing with exactly the buyers that matter.

## Stance
Lending through curated vaults when rates beat Aave — that's the practical
edge of permissionless market creation: rate discovery happens faster in
isolated markets. No MORPHO token position: ~$192M annualized fees with
zero attributed to tokenholders is the "strong product, weak token"
problem, and the ~22.6% unlock overhang makes it worse. Product yes,
token no, until value accrual switches on.

## Risks
- Curator model moves risk, doesn't remove it — a bad curator with high caps is a pooled-risk protocol wearing a costume
- Unlock overhang (~22.6% cited) with thin 24h volume ($17M)
- Embedded-lending concentration: Coinbase is the flagship and the dependency
- 38-chain sprawl means long-tail deployments with near-zero liquidity carrying the brand
