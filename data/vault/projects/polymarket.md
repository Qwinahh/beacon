# Polymarket

## Thesis
Polymarket won the brand war and is losing the volume war. May 2026:
~$7.1B vs Kalshi's $17.9B — Kalshi out-traded it 2.5x, and Polymarket
volume fell two months straight ($10.5B Mar → $9B Apr → $7.1B May, CNBC
2026-06-10). The mix explains it: sports is ~80% of Kalshi's volume vs
~39% of Polymarket's, and sports is where the volume lives (sector-wide
~$24B/month by April, Pew). The World Cup is Polymarket's counterpunch —
>$1.9B across 328 markets already. Meanwhile the CFTC's 267-page NPRM
(2026-06-10) would legalize sports contracts federally and ban
war/assassination markets: regulation is arriving as a sorting mechanism,
not a ban hammer.

## Stance
Farming the airdrop, modestly. POLY is confirmed planned (CMO, Oct 2025)
but NOT launched — no token, no tokenomics, anyone quoting a POLY price
is reading fan fiction. H2 2026 is the community guess. Position: real
volume on markets I have a view on (catalyst markets, not coin-flip
sports), because wash-farming patterns are exactly what an airdrop
criteria team learns to filter. The Polymarket USD settlement transition
this spring is also a farming-relevant signal — they're building their
own money rail pre-token.

## Risks
- Kalshi momentum compounds — regulated, US-native, sports-first is simply a bigger TAM than event markets
- Trump Jr. board seat / 1789 Capital stake invites the political-conflict investigation already requested (Democracy Defenders Fund, Jun 9)
- CFTC NPRM cuts both ways: sports legalization helps Kalshi more than Polymarket
- Airdrop timing/criteria are pure speculation — farming cost must stay near zero
