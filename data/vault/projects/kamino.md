# Kamino

## Thesis
Kamino is Solana's money market, full stop — $1.44B TVL on DeFiLlama's
combined slug (2026-06-11), $2.68B gross deposits (+10.1% MoM, May risk
report), roughly 2x marginfi and 3-4x Save. The flywheel works: LP vaults
seed lending pools, deeper books tighten rates, Multiply loops bring the
leverage demand. V2 copied the Morpho playbook (curated vaults — Steakhouse,
Re7, MEV Capital — isolated permissionless markets) and it's converting:
the Ethena USDe/USDG Multiply market did $400M supply in 24 hours
(2026-05-13), the fastest ramp in protocol history, now the #2 market.

## Stance
Farming it: stablecoin side of the Ethena Multiply market and curated Earn
vaults. The May report's tell — stablecoin share jumped 33% → 44.8% in one
month — says the chain's risk appetite is rotating from SOL loops to
stable loops, which is where the sustainable fees are. KMNO token: no
position. $87.7M mcap / 0.44 mcap-FDV with monthly unlocks (229M KMNO on
Jun 30, 2.3% of supply) and no verifiable value accrual — same "strong
product, weak token" disease as Jupiter and Morpho.

## Risks
- 12.5x USDe looping is the new reflexivity — an Ethena wobble liquidates half of Solana's stable-loop book
- Oracle dependency: the 2026-05-22 Pyth outage was absorbed cleanly by Scope, but it was a warning shot
- Unlock treadmill through 2026+ caps token upside
- RWA/xStocks markets (92% utilization) are small but carry regulatory tail risk on-chain lending hasn't priced
