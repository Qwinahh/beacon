# GMX

## Thesis
GMX is what a perps DEX looks like after it loses the war but keeps the
business. GLP is dead — V1 sunset July 2025, $2.85M residual vs $161M in v2
GM/GLV pools (DeFiLlama, 2026-06-11), a 98/2 split. The "real yield" thesis
didn't die, it mutated: 27% of fees now buy back GMX instead of paying ETH
to stakers. ~$842K holders revenue over 30d against a $57M mcap is a
low-double-digit annualized accrual — mechanically real, just on a fee base
that shrank an order of magnitude from the 2021–22 era.

## Stance
No position. The fee math is honest but the volume war is over —
Hyperliquid holds ~70–80% of perp DEX share and GMX's 30d fees are $2.23M
total. The optionality is commodity perps (gold/oil/gas launched 2026-05-08)
and the MegaETH deployment (8th chain, early June). If RWA perps find
volume nobody else has, the buyback flywheel on a $57M mcap gets
interesting fast. Until then it's a value-coin argument, and value
arguments in perps have been losing for two years.

## Risks
- Fee base keeps shrinking — $208K/24h fees doesn't fund relevance
- The reported "staking rewards paused until $90" gambit (single source, UNVERIFIED — do not post as fact) would be a desperation signal if confirmed
- Multichain sprawl (8 networks) spreads thin liquidity thinner
- July 2025 GLP hack + $44M comp plan already burned the LP base once
