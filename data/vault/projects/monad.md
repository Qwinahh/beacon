# Monad

## Thesis
Monad shipped the tech and the demand didn't show. Mainnet live since
2025-11-24, 10,000 TPS capacity, 0.4s blocks — running at roughly 21 TPS
sustained average over its first ~78 days (~0.07% utilization per
third-party estimates) and ~$4k/day in gas fees (DeFiLlama, 2026-06-11).
TVL ~$350-360M is real but it's lending-vault mercenary capital: Morpho
$96M, Euler $92M, K3 $61M — curators chasing the Monad Momentum matching
program, not users. Native apps (Kuru CLOB at ~$1.4M TVL, Perpl) are
rounding errors. The parallel-EVM thesis was "build faster rails and
demand follows." Demand has not followed yet.

## Stance
No MON position, not farming. Airdrop done (Oct-Nov 2025, ~289k wallets).
MON at $0.0215 / $255M mcap / ~$2.15B FDV with ~47% of supply starting
monthly vesting Nov 2026 is a structurally heavy chart. The honest bull
case is institutional plumbing (Open Transaction Layer, Centrifuge,
Tether Gold) finding product-market fit before the unlock cliff —
possible, not probable. Watching gas fees as the only demand metric
that can't be bought.

## Risks
- Nov 2026 unlock cliff: ~47% of supply vesting monthly into 2029
- Incentive-driven TVL leaves when matching programs end
- "Speed war" with MegaETH compresses the differentiation story from both sides
- Capacity at 0.07% utilization makes every performance upgrade (MIP-12, +25% speed) a solution without a problem
