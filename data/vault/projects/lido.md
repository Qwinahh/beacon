# Lido

## Thesis
Lido is the most profitable protocol in DeFi that pays its token nothing.
$18.67B TVL, $45.2M fees in 30 days (~$551M annualized, 2026-06-12,
DeFiLlama) — and LDO holders receive exactly $0 of it, which is why LDO
trades at ~$0.27-0.34, ~96% off ATH, near its listed all-time low. Market
share is flat-to-down from the >30% era to ~24-28% of staked ETH; the
centralization critique is aging but not dead — CSM + SimpleDVT, the
permissionless/DVT answer, is still only 2.2% of all ETH stake (Lido's
own Q4 2025 metrics). The 2026 pivot is the real story: Staking Router v3
(LIP-35, mainnet ~July), stVaults targeting 1M ETH, ValMart validator
marketplace — staking infra trying to become a platform.

## Stance
Hold stETH as the default ETH staking position — deepest liquidity, 2.36%
vs rETH's 1.99%, and Dual Governance (stETH-holder veto) is an underrated
structural protection. No LDO: a governance token over $551M of fees it
can't touch is an option on a fee switch that DAO politics may never
exercise. Rocket Pool comparison is settled for now: 16.5x smaller,
lower yield, more decentralized — a philosophy, not a competitor.

## Risks
- Fee-switch expectations could break either way: turning it on invites regulatory attention, leaving it off keeps LDO a zombie
- ETH staking yield compression (2.36% and drifting down) shrinks the whole category's revenue base
- Staking Router v3 migration (through ~Q1 2027) is a long, complex surgery on $18B of live stake
- DVT modules growing too slowly to retire the centralization narrative before regulators notice it
