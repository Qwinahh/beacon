# Yield-Bearing Stables

## Thesis
The yield-bearing stable meta survived its crash and came out boring —
which is the bullish version. USDe imploded from $14.6B to $4.48B after
Oct 10, 2025, then rebuilt as a 97.4%-cash-backed product yielding 3.5%
(2026-06-11, Ethena API). The meta-shift: yield sources rotated from
crypto-native (funding, points) to TradFi-native (T-bills, CLOs, lending
books). USDY pays 4.65%, sUSDe 3.5%, sUSDe PT on [[projects/pendle|Pendle]]
locks just 4.31% — the whole curve compressed to T-bill-plus-epsilon.
When the premium is 100bps, distribution beats yield: that's why the
Coinbase savings product (Ethena-powered) and JupUSD-in-every-Jupiter-
product matter more than any APY table. The next leg up only comes when
funding regimes turn and the crypto-native carry returns at size.

## What to watch
- sUSDe APY vs 3-month T-bill spread — the meta's heartbeat (currently ~0-50bps, dead)
