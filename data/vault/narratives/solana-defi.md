# Solana DeFi

## Thesis
Solana DeFi consolidated into a two-protocol economy: [[projects/jupiter|Jupiter]]
routes the flow ($17.06B 30d aggregator volume, 93.6% of aggregator-routed
DEX flow at last reading), [[projects/kamino|Kamino]] holds the capital
($1.44B TVL, $2.68B gross deposits — a quarter-plus of the chain's ~$5.5B
DeFi TVL). What makes Solana DeFi different from Ethereum's isn't speed,
it's integration topology: one aggregator, one money market, shared
oracle infra (Pyth/Scope), and now one stablecoin attempt (JupUSD,
BUIDL-backed) — vertical integration that Ethereum's fragmented L2
landscape structurally can't replicate. The third pillar fell over:
[[projects/drift|Drift]] was hacked for $295M (Apr 1, DPRK-attributed)
and remains suspended, its relaunch goal downgraded to "largest
USDT-based perp DEX on Solana." The chain absorbed a nine-figure exploit
of its #3 DeFi protocol with zero contagion — Kamino's May liquidations
fell 95% MoM — which is quietly the strongest resilience datapoint
Solana has ever printed. SOL at ~$87.7 (2026-06-11) prices none of this.
