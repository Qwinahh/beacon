# Restaking / EigenLayer

## Thesis
Restaking is a structural primitive, not a narrative. EigenLayer proved
the demand side: $15B+ in restaked ETH at peak. The open question is
whether AVS operators can generate enough fee revenue to justify the
additional slashing risk taken by restakers.

The bet is on the supply side winning before the demand side matures —
points farming and restaking yield are funding the ecosystem's growth
phase. Whether that transitions to real economic activity is the thesis.

## What to watch
- AVS count vs AVS fee revenue (key divergence — many AVSs, near-zero fees)
- EigenLayer restaking yield vs native ETH staking yield (the spread matters)
- EIGEN token unlock schedule and sell pressure
- LRT (Liquid Restaking Token) TVL flows — where capital is moving

## Key projects
- [[projects/eigenlayer|EigenLayer]] — the base protocol, trust score 3
- EtherFi — largest LRT by TVL, watching closely
- Symbiotic — competitor restaking layer, gaining ground

## Signal patterns
- AVS launching without real fee model → contrarian post opportunity
- Restaking yield dropping below 4% → capital rotation signal
- New LRT launching → crowded, post with caution flag

## Risks
- Systemic slashing event cascading through LRTs
- AVS demand never materialises — restaking yield becomes purely inflationary
- Regulatory targeting of pooled staking arrangements
