# Modular Blockchains

## Thesis
The modular thesis won technically and lost economically. Celestia
shipped everything it promised — Matcha upgrade (Jan 2026) with 128MB
blocks, node storage cut 77%, issuance slashed from 5% to 0.25% via
Proof-of-Governance, ~50% DA market share — and the revenue is $62.80
per day in fees (CoinMarketCap, Jun 2026). Not $62.80 million. Dollars.
TIA sits at ~$0.32-0.40, mcap ~$368M (Jun 1, 2026), down from a $20B+
FDV peak. The lesson: DA became infinitely abundant the moment three
providers (Celestia, EigenDA, Ethereum blobs) competed for the same
rollups — EigenDA prices 100MB/day at ~$730/year vs Celestia's ~$12,775.
Selling blockspace whose marginal cost rounds to zero is a commodity
business, and commodity businesses don't support monetary-premium
valuations. EigenLayer's restaking variant is faring no better:
EigenCloud TVL −37.1% over 30d (DeFiLlama top 20, 2026-06-12), worst
in the entire top 20.

## What to watch
- Celestia daily fees — the honest demand metric; 10x growth still lands at trivial absolutes
- EigenCloud TVL decay rate and whether any AVS generates real fee flow to restakers
- 1GB block rollout: more supply into a market with no demand pressure
- Any rollup paying meaningful DA fees at scale (none today)
- [[projects/monad|Monad]] as the integrated counter-thesis: monolithic performance also found no demand — the problem may be demand itself, not architecture

## Key projects
