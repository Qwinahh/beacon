# Prediction Markets

## Thesis
Prediction markets graduated from election-night toy to a ~$24B/month
sector (Pew, April 2026) — and crypto is losing the category it
invented. Kalshi (regulated, US-native) did >$17.9B in May vs
[[projects/polymarket|Polymarket]]'s ~$7.1B, out-trading it 2.5x while
Polymarket fell two straight months. The mix is the moat: sports is ~80%
of Kalshi's volume vs ~39% of Polymarket's, and sports is where retail
lives. The CFTC's 267-page NPRM (2026-06-10) formalizes the sorting:
sports contracts legal, war/assassination banned. POLY token still
doesn't exist — anyone quoting a price is wrong. Value capture remains
the open question: Polymarket's real asset is that CNBC and Bloomberg
cite its odds as data infrastructure, and "reference odds feed" is a
different business from a sportsbook. Crypto's actual wedge now is
embedding: [[projects/jupiter|Jupiter]]'s Forecast (Jun 5) — native
prediction markets inside a DEX superapp, settled in JupUSD.
