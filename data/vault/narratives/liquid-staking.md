# Liquid Staking

## Thesis
Liquid staking is a mature oligopoly with one open question: who
captures the institutional wave. [[projects/lido|Lido]] holds ~24-28% of
staked ETH ($18.67B TVL, 2026-06-12) — flat-to-down from the >30% era,
which quietly retired the cartel-panic discourse. Binance staked ETH is
#2 at ~$6-8B. Rocket Pool is a $1.13B philosophy project — 16.5x smaller
than Lido, lower yield (1.99% vs 2.36%), more decentralized, no growth.
The DVT "threat" became a feature: Lido absorbed it (SimpleDVT + CSM =
2.2% of all ETH stake), and SSV Network sits at $12.21B TVL — #2 in all
of DeFi — as the invisible infrastructure layer almost nobody discusses.
Where the alpha still is: the institutional wrapper layer (WisdomTree
stETH fund, Hex Trust custody) and the Lido V3 platform pivot (stVaults
targeting 1M ETH, ValMart, LIP-35 Staking Router v3 mainnet ~July 2026).
