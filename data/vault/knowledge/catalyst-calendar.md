## How Token Unlocks Work as Catalysts

Unlock events are the most predictable recurring catalyst in crypto because they are
scheduled in advance, visible on-chain, and mechanically create supply. The signal
is not that unlocks always dump price — it is that unlocks shift the supply/demand
balance in a predictable window, and the net effect depends on context:

- **Unlock + no buyback mechanism + falling TVL = highest risk.** All three selling
  pressures compound. This was the pattern in most 2024-era L2 unlock dumps.
- **Unlock + active buyback + growing protocol revenue = ambiguous.** The HYPE AQA v2
  setup post-October 2026 is this case. Whether buybacks outpace unlock sell pressure
  requires doing the actual dollar math, not reading CT takes.
- **Unlock into a flight-to-quality regime** (current, June 2026) generally means less
  marginal buying to absorb the supply. Unlocks land harder when new capital is not
  entering the category.

The post-worthy angle on any unlock: state the size, state the current buyback rate
or mechanism, state the net float implication. Three numbers, no adjectives.

## How to use this in posts

1. **Pre-unlock framing.** 7-10 days before a known unlock, post the math: size, current
   price, what percent of circulating supply, and what (if any) buyback mechanism exists.
   "9.92M HYPE unlocks June 6. At current price that is $565M and 2.54% of released supply.
   AQA buyback is the offset — watching whether the run rate covers it." No prediction,
   just the numbers.

2. **Post-unlock review.** After a major unlock, post whether price absorbed it and what
   the HLP/TVL data showed during the window. The follow-through is more useful than
   the pre-event take.

3. **Kalshi filing as regulatory signal.** When the HYPE perpetuals decision comes from
   regulators, the post is not about HYPE specifically — it is about what a regulated
   futures product on a perp DEX's native token means for the category.

4. **AQA v2 as a buyback model case study.** When Oct 3, 2026 approaches, frame the
   AQA v2 activation as a live experiment: can a protocol's own revenue structurally
   offset token unlock pressure? That is a testable thesis, worth pre-announcing and
   tracking in real time.
