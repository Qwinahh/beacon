# Protocol Patterns — What Separates Signal from Noise

Reference for the writer when evaluating whether a protocol deserves a post
and what angle to take. Grounded in observable on-chain and market history.

---

## High-Signal Launch Patterns

### The Paradigm Signal
If Paradigm leads a round, it's a tier-1 signal. Paradigm doesn't do
headline-grab rounds. They invest early in protocols with novel mechanisms.
Track record: Uniswap, Optimism, Blur, Hyperliquid (indirect).

### Fair Launch Premium
No VC allocation + community distribution = higher community trust and
lower near-term sell pressure from VCs with 1-year cliffs. Examples:
YFI, Hyperliquid HYPE. Posts about fair launches land differently — frame
around what the lack of VC means, not just the airdrop size.

### TVL Velocity > TVL Size
A protocol growing TVL 5x in 30 days on $50M is more interesting than a
protocol sitting at $2B flat. The early mover window is where alpha is.
Use DeFiLlama 7d change data, not snapshot TVL.

### Audit + Age Combo
Audited protocols with 12+ months live > unaudited new launches regardless
of yield. Post about unaudited high-yield opportunities with explicit risk
framing. Never write about them as straightforward opportunities.

## Red Flags — What to Avoid Posting About Uncritically

- **Token before product**: Token launched, mainnet "coming soon". High risk.
- **Algorithmic stablecoin**: Post-LUNA, this phrase is a soft red flag.
  Exception: overcollateralised stables (DAI model) are different.
- **Anonymous team + no audit**: Two risk factors together = noise tier.
- **APY > 200% "sustainable"**: Inflationary tokenomics dressed as yield.
  Almost always compress to zero. Frame as such.
- **"Backed by X" without specifics**: Ghost VC names or unnamed angels.
  Check actual fund on-chain or via Crunchbase before writing.

## Airdrop Farming — Conviction Framework

Use this when the Researcher evaluates whether a farm is worth pursuing.

| Factor | Weight | Question |
|--------|--------|----------|
| TVL    | High   | > $5M TVL before token? |
| VC backing | High | Is there credible VC with track record? |
| Usage growth | High | Is daily active usage trending up? |
| Competition | Medium | Is this the clear leader in its niche? |
| Audit  | Medium | Is the code audited by a named firm? |
| Token supply | Low | Is founder/VC allocation < 30%? |

3+ positives = worth farming. Write with specific actions.
2 or fewer = watching, not farming. Note the gaps.

## Narrative Timing

Narratives run in 3-9 month windows. The optimal entry point for posts is
when the narrative is forming, not when it's saturated:

- **AI agents** (late 2024): Was Virtuals Protocol on Base. Saturated by mid-2025.
- **Restaking** (2023-2024): EigenLayer TVL surge. Saturated post-EIGEN TGE.
- **Perps DEX** (2023-ongoing): Still early relative to CEX market share.
- **RWA / tokenised treasuries** (2023-ongoing): Blackrock BUIDL, Ondo. Still forming.

Post about a narrative when fewer than 5 major accounts are calling it.
Once it's consensus, pivot to the second-order question ("who wins within this?").
