# Reply Strategy — Drafting Replies That Build Relationships

*IMPORTANT: replies in this system are HUMAN-APPROVED — the bot drafts,
Quin reviews and sends. This file is guidance for draft QUALITY, not
autonomous volume. Every draft should be good enough that Quin sends it
unedited; volume is irrelevant.*

## Why replies are the growth engine

The algorithm math ([[knowledge/x-algorithm-2026]]): a reply the author
engages with carries a +75-class signal — 150x a like — and exposes our
account to the author's entire audience. For a small account, one
author-engaged reply on a 100k-follower account out-distributes a week
of our own timeline posts. Replies also rank under the post by predicted
engagement, with Premium replies prioritized — being early and
substantive puts us at the top of a high-traffic conversation.

## Account prioritization

Tier 1 — analysts whose audience matches ours (reply when we can add data):
@DefiIgnas, @thedefiedge, @Route2FI — data-thread writers whose followers
bookmark and follow analysts. An author-engaged reply here converts.

Tier 2 — large personalities (reply only with exceptional material):
@Pentosh1, @0xSisyphus class accounts. Huge reach, low author-engagement
odds, reply stacks are brutal. Only draft when we have a number that
genuinely extends or contradicts the post.

Tier 3 — mid-size protocol researchers and risk teams (10k-100k):
highest relationship ROI. These accounts read their replies, remember
recurring names, and RT good additions. This is where multi-reply
relationship arcs actually work.

Skip: engagement-bait accounts, giveaway accounts, anything where our
reply sits next to 400 "gm ser" replies.

## What to add (the only four moves worth drafting)

1. **The missing number.** Post says "Hyperliquid OI ripping" → reply adds "$5.88B as of this morning, and HLP TVL flat at $336M — the depth is keeping up." Extends, doesn't repeat.
2. **The documented contradiction.** Post claims X, our vault says not-X with a source → push back, politely, with the number. Our stance files exist for this. Disagreement with receipts is the highest-engagement reply class.
3. **The precedent.** "Same setup as [event, year] — that time it resolved [outcome]." Pattern memory is rare in reply sections.
4. **The sharp question only we'd ask.** A question that demonstrates expertise ("is that net of the looped USDe in Kamino's number?") — invites author response, which is the +75 jackpot.

## What never to draft

- Agreement without addition ("this", "100%", "great thread ser")
- Templated anything — Grox spam classifiers profile repeated reply patterns; every draft must be post-specific
- Repeated @mentions of the same handle — explicitly a suppression label; vary targets across the week
- Questions that reveal we didn't read the post
- Self-promotion, links to our own posts, "I wrote about this" (the profile click does that job silently)
- Corrections of typos or trivia; only correct substance
- Anything in a reply we wouldn't post on the timeline (replies are indexed and screenshot-able)

## Multi-reply relationship arcs

The goal with a Tier 3 account is recognition, not a single banger.
The arc that converts to a follow-back and eventual mutual:

1. **Week 1-2:** two or three data-adding replies. No questions yet. Goal: name recognition ("the person who always has the number").
2. **Week 2-4:** one respectful pushback with receipts. Disagreement registers harder than agreement once trust exists.
3. **Ongoing:** reference their earlier point in a later reply ("this is the unwind you flagged in May"). Demonstrating memory of their work is the strongest relationship signal that exists in reply sections.
4. Never two replies on the same author's posts in one day. Presence ≠ omnipresence.

## Patterns that convert to follows (observed)

- Being the reply with the live number when the post has a stale one
- The "actually it's worse/better than that" extension — same thesis, sharper data
- Calm correction of a widely-RT'd wrong figure (our wrong_take_correction format works in replies too)
- Early reply (within ~2h, recency matters in notification ordering) that the author likes → their audience sees us at the top all day

## Draft quality bar (judge before surfacing to Quin)

Every draft must pass: (1) contains a specific number, named mechanic,
or documented precedent; (2) consistent with our vault stance on the
protocol (load the project file's Stance + X Consensus); (3) reads like
[[persona]] voice, passes [[knowledge/voice-integrity]]; (4) would make
the author more likely to engage, not defensive.
