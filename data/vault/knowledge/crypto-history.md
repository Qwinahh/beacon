# Crypto History — Major Events 2017–2025

Reference knowledge for the bot. All facts here are confirmed historical record.
The bot reads this file when generating posts referencing past events, cycles, and precedents.

---

## 2017 — ICO Mania & Bitcoin's First Mainstream Moment

- **Jan 2017**: BTC ~$1,000. ETH ~$8.
- **The ICO Wave**: Ethereum enabled token launches at zero friction. Hundreds of projects raised millions with a whitepaper. EOS raised $4B over a year-long ICO. Filecoin raised $257M.
- **Bitcoin hits $20K (Dec 17, 2017)**: ATH at the time. Futures launched on CME/CBOE, institutional money entered via futures — mostly to short.
- **Bitcoin Cash fork (Aug 2017)**: Roger Ver / Bitmain fork war. BCH split from BTC over block size. Signalled governance as a real attack surface.
- **Crypto kitties (Nov 2017)**: First viral NFT/on-chain game. Clogged Ethereum. Gas fees spiked. Proved ETH couldn't scale at the time.
- **Key lesson**: Narrative (ICO mania) drives retail, futures shorting drives institutional entry. Cycle ends when narrative exhausts.

## 2018 — The Bear Market

- **Jan 2018**: BTC peaks ~$17K, dumps to ~$3,200 by Dec.
- **SEC cracks down**: Most ICOs deemed securities. Dozens of enforcement actions. Projects that raised $100M+ went to zero.
- **Mt. Gox claims active**: Legal proceedings through 2018, eventually resolved in 2024 with BTC distributions.
- **Bitmain IPO fails (Oct 2018)**: Filed for HK IPO, failed amid bear market. Bitmain lost $740M in a single quarter from BCH holdings.
- **USDC launched (Sep 2018)**: Circle + Coinbase launch USDC as regulated stablecoin alternative to Tether.
- **Key lesson**: Every cycle kills the projects that were riding narrative, not utility. Stablecoins deepen during bear as people preserve capital.

## 2019 — DeFi Seeds Planted

- **IEO wave**: Exchanges (Binance Launchpad) replaced ICOs. BitTorrent (BTT) raised $7.2M in 18 minutes on Launchpad in Jan 2019.
- **Compound launches (Sep 2018, gains traction 2019)**: First protocol-level lending. Borrowing crypto to lever up.
- **Synthetix (rebranded Jan 2019)**: Synthetic assets on Ethereum. Early DeFi building block.
- **Uniswap V1 launches (Nov 2018)**: AMM concept proven. By 2019 it had real volume.
- **Binance Smart Chain announced**: CZ signaled intent to build an ETH-compatible chain under Binance control.
- **Facebook Libra announced (Jun 2019)**: Regulatory firestorm. US Congress summoned Zuckerberg. Killed by regulators before launch. Later became Diem, then quietly shut down in 2022.
- **Key lesson**: Bear markets are when the actual infrastructure gets built. DeFi primitives (AMM, lending, synths) all emerged in the depths of the 2018-2019 bear.

## 2020 — DeFi Summer & COVID Shock

- **Mar 2020 COVID crash**: BTC dropped from ~$9K to ~$3,800 in hours. Mass liquidations. BitMEX processed record volume. Then recovered faster than any traditional asset.
- **DeFi Summer (Jun–Sep 2020)**: Compound launches COMP governance token with liquidity mining (Jun 2020). Triggered yield farming mania. TVL went from $1B to $15B in months.
- **Yearn Finance (Jul 2020)**: Andre Cronje launches YFI with zero pre-mine, zero VC allocation. Community token. YFI went from $0 to $40,000+ in weeks. First truly "fair launch."
- **SushiSwap vampire attack (Aug 2020)**: Forked Uniswap, migrated $1B+ in liquidity via SUSHI incentives. Chef Nomi (anon founder) rug pulled $14M then returned it.
- **Uniswap V2 + retroactive UNI airdrop (Sep 2020)**: Anyone who had ever used Uniswap got 400 UNI (~$1,200 at launch). Set the template for airdrop farming.
- **Bitcoin halving (May 2020)**: Block reward cut from 12.5 to 6.25 BTC. Third halving. Historically preceded bull runs.
- **MicroStrategy buys BTC (Aug 2020)**: Michael Saylor announces $250M BTC purchase as treasury reserve. First major public company to do so.
- **Key lesson**: Liquidity mining (yield farming) bootstraps protocols but creates mercenary capital. Fair launches create loyalty. Airdrops to past users set the farming playbook still used today.

## 2021 — NFT Mania, L2s, and the Peak

- **BTC hits $64K (Apr 2021)**: Coinbase IPO triggers ATH. Institutional narrative fully mainstream.
- **NFT explosion**: Beeple sells "Everydays" at Christie's for $69M (Mar 2021). OpenSea volume explodes. NBA Top Shot, Axie Infinity, CryptoPunks go mainstream.
- **Axie Infinity (peak summer 2021)**: Play-to-earn. Philippines economy partially runs on AXS/SLP. Proved game economies could onboard millions. Also proved they were Ponzis — SLP collapsed when new players dried up.
- **Polygon (MATIC) rise**: Cheap ETH alternative. TVL grew 10x in months. Hundreds of DeFi forks deployed there for cheap gas.
- **Solana emerges (H1 2021)**: Fast, cheap, VC-backed. Phantom wallet launches. Serum (FTX-backed DEX) anchors DeFi. SOL goes from $2 to $260 in the bull.
- **China mining ban (May/Jun 2021)**: Hash rate drops 50%+ overnight. BTC drops from $64K to $29K. Miners migrate to US, Kazakhstan, others.
- **BTC hits $69K ATH (Nov 2021)**: New ATH. ProShares Bitcoin ETF (futures-based) launches on NYSE. ETH also ATH at ~$4,800.
- **Terra/Luna early rise**: Do Kwon builds UST/LUNA algorithmic stablecoin. TVL in Anchor Protocol (19.5% yield on UST) pulls billions.
- **Key lesson**: NFTs proved digital ownership. Play-to-earn proved it doesn't work without external capital inflows. L2/alt L1s emerged as ETH scaling solutions during gas fee pain.

## 2022 — The Cascade: Terra, 3AC, Celsius, FTX

- **Terra/Luna collapse (May 2022)**: UST de-pegged. LUNA hyperinflated to restore peg. $60B evaporated in days. Do Kwon fled, eventually arrested in Montenegro (2023). Anchor's 19.5% yield was subsidized — unsustainable by design.
- **Three Arrows Capital (3AC) insolvency (Jun 2022)**: Su Zhu and Kyle Davies ran $10B+ fund with extreme leverage. Post-Luna, they couldn't meet margin calls. Filed for bankruptcy. Su Zhu later arrested in Singapore airport (Sep 2023).
- **Celsius collapse (Jun 2022)**: Retail "bank" offering 18% yield on crypto. Froze withdrawals Jun 12, filed bankruptcy Jul 13. CEO Alex Mashinsky arrested (Jul 2023), pleaded guilty (2024).
- **Voyager Digital bankruptcy (Jul 2022)**: Lender with heavy 3AC exposure. Filed Chapter 11.
- **FTX collapse (Nov 2022)**: CoinDesk publishes Alameda balance sheet (Nov 2). Binance announces selling FTX token (FTT). Bank run ensues. $8B hole discovered. Sam Bankman-Fried arrested Dec 12. Convicted on all 7 counts (Nov 2023). Sentenced to 25 years (Mar 2024).
- **BTC bottoms at ~$15.5K (Nov 2022)**: Cycle low. Fear & Greed index hits single digits.
- **Key lesson**: Yield that sounds too good to always is. Contagion moves fast in crypto — Luna→3AC→Celsius→Voyager→FTX all within 6 months. Centralized custodians (not your keys, not your coins) carry counterparty risk.

## 2023 — Regulation, Recovery & the AI Narrative

- **Silvergate / Signature / SVB banks fail (Mar 2023)**: Three crypto-friendly banks collapse in a week. USDC briefly de-pegged to $0.87 (Circle had $3.3B in SVB). Biggest stablecoin scare since UST.
- **SEC sues Coinbase and Binance (Jun 2023)**: Gensler labels most altcoins securities. Binance pays $4.3B settlement (Nov 2023). CZ pleads guilty, steps down as CEO.
- **BTC ETF applications heat up**: BlackRock files spot BTC ETF (Jun 2023). Changes the regulatory narrative — if BlackRock is applying, approval seems inevitable.
- **Ordinals / BRC-20 (Jan 2023)**: Bitcoin NFTs via inscriptions. Drove BTC fees to multi-year highs. Reignited debate about Bitcoin's use case.
- **AI narrative captures crypto**: ChatGPT (Nov 2022) has tail effect in 2023. AI tokens (FET, AGIX, OCEAN merge into ASI) pump. Worldcoin launches (Jul 2023). AI+crypto narrative established.
- **Arbitrum ARB airdrop (Mar 2023)**: 12.75% of supply to early users. Largest L2 airdrop to date. Farming L2s becomes mainstream strategy.
- **zkSync, StarkNet, Scroll farm season begins**: Millions bridge, transact, deploy contracts hoping for airdrops. zkSync Era mainnet opens (Mar 2023).
- **Key lesson**: Regulatory clarity (even negative) eventually resolves into market recovery. Institutional ETF narrative is a multi-year tailwind. Airdrops are now a professional sport.

## 2024 — ETFs, Halving, and the New Cycle

- **Spot BTC ETF approved (Jan 11, 2024)**: SEC approves 11 spot BTC ETFs simultaneously. BlackRock's IBIT becomes fastest ETF to $10B AUM ever (within weeks). $4.6B inflows on day one. Massive demand vs. anticipated.
- **Bitcoin halving (Apr 20, 2024)**: Fourth halving. Block reward → 3.125 BTC. Unlike past cycles, price already near ATH before halving (unusual).
- **BTC hits $73K ATH (Mar 2024)**: Pre-halving rally driven by ETF inflows. Then corrects to ~$57K, then resumes.
- **Ethereum spot ETF approved (May 2024)**: Surprise approval. ETH ETFs launch Jul 23, 2024.
- **Mt. Gox distributions begin (Jul 2024)**: After 10 years, creditors finally receive BTC/BCH. ~142K BTC distributed. Markets feared sell pressure — actual impact was muted.
- **zkSync airdrop (Jun 2024)**: Long-awaited. Controversial — many farmers received less than expected due to Sybil filtering. Set off debate about farming strategies.
- **Eigen Layer airdrop (2024)**: Restaking protocol. Largest airdrop in absolute dollar terms at time of distribution for some recipients.
- **LayerZero airdrop (Jun 2024)**: Used self-reporting Sybil system — users could report other wallets for a reward. Created chaos and controversy.
- **Trump elected (Nov 2024)**: Republican sweep. BTC surges to $100K+ within weeks. Pro-crypto cabinet picks (Paul Atkins as SEC chair, David Sacks as crypto czar).
- **BTC hits $100K (Dec 2024)**: Psychological milestone. Retail FOMO returns. Memecoins explode.
- **Key lesson**: ETF approval was a multi-year institutional unlock. Halving supply shock thesis confirmed again. Airdrop farming is now adversarial — projects use Sybil filtering, self-reporting, activity requirements.

## 2025 — Institutional Era & Solana Season

- **BTC ATH ~$109K (Jan 2025)**: Trump inauguration week rally.
- **Solana memecoin supercycle**: TRUMP and MELANIA official memecoins launch hours before inauguration. Billions in volume. Pump.fun dominates token launches. Solana processes more transactions than all other chains combined during peak.
- **Strategic Bitcoin Reserve proposed (Mar 2025)**: Trump signs executive order directing Treasury to establish BTC reserve from forfeited assets. ~200K BTC held by US government.
- **Hyperliquid becomes dominant perps DEX**: $10B+ in daily volume at peak. HYPE token airdrop (Nov 2024) worth $28K+ per address for top farmers. Distributed $1.8B to community.
- **Bybit hack (Feb 2025)**: $1.5B ETH stolen by North Korea's Lazarus Group — largest crypto hack in history. Bybit survived, bought back the stolen ETH, remained solvent.
- **Ethereum pectra upgrade (May 2025)**: Account abstraction, validator improvements. Staking experience improves significantly.
- **Real-world assets (RWAs) go mainstream**: BlackRock's BUIDL fund ($500M+ on-chain). Tokenized T-bills become significant DeFi collateral.
- **Key lesson**: Institutional custody + ETFs + regulatory clarity = new baseline demand. Memecoins are now their own sector with real volume. Solana wins consumer/retail; Ethereum wins institutional/RWA.

## Cross-Cycle Patterns

| Pattern | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Bear builds infra | DeFi (2018-19), L2s (2022-23), RWAs (2023-24) |
| Halving → bull (6-18mo lag) | 2012→2013, 2016→2017, 2020→2021, 2024→2025 |
| Airdrop standards tighten each cycle | Uni→ARB→zkSync→EigenLayer: each one harder to farm |
| Leverage unwinds fast | 3AC/Celsius/FTX all within one year |
| BTC leads, alts follow (with lag) | Each cycle: BTC ATH → ETH follows → alts follow |
| Narrative precedes price | AI tokens (2023), RWA (2024), before revenue justified it |
| Rug pattern | Anon founders + no audit + high APY = red flags |
