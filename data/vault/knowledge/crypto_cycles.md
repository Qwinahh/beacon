# Crypto Market Cycles — 2017–2025

Reference knowledge for the bot's writer. Use to contextualise current market
conditions against historical patterns.

---

## 2017 — First Retail Cycle

- BTC peaked ~$20K in December 2017. ICO boom drove altcoin speculation.
- ETH rose from ~$8 to ~$1,400. ERC-20 token issuance exploded.
- Catalysts: Coinbase growth, mainstream media coverage, ICO mania.
- Correction: -84% BTC drawdown from peak to Jan 2019 trough (~$3,200).
- Key lesson: narrative precedes fundamentals in retail cycles.

## 2020–2021 — DeFi Summer → NFT → L1 Wars

- DeFi Summer (Jun–Sep 2020): Compound liquidity mining sparked yield farming.
  TVL went from ~$1B to ~$15B by end of 2020.
- BTC hit $64K in April 2021, corrected -56%, recovered to $69K in Nov 2021.
- ETH ATH: ~$4,900 (Nov 2021).
- Alt-L1 boom: Solana, Avalanche, Fantom, NEAR surged on EVM congestion/fee narrative.
- NFT boom: OpenSea volume peaked Aug 2021 (~$3.4B/month).
- Catalyst shift: institutional entry (MicroStrategy, Tesla, Coinbase IPO).
- Correction: LUNA/UST collapse (May 2022) began -80%+ bear market.

## 2022 — Bear Market

- LUNA/UST implosion (May 2022): $40B wiped, contagion hit 3AC, Celsius, Voyager.
- FTX collapse (Nov 2022): ~$8B in customer funds missing. BTC fell to ~$15,500.
- Total crypto market cap: $3T (Nov 2021) → $750B (Nov 2022).
- DeFi TVL: $180B (Jan 2022) → $37B (Dec 2022).
- Key lesson: CeFi leverage and collateral opacity amplify drawdowns.

## 2023 — Recovery & L2 Narrative

- BTC recovered from ~$15.5K to ~$44K by year-end (+180%).
- Liquid staking derivatives (Lido, Rocket Pool) grew post-Shanghai upgrade (Apr 2023).
- L2s (Arbitrum, Optimism) launched tokens. Base launched (Aug 2023, no token).
- Airdrop meta became dominant: users farmed protocols pre-TGE.
- Ordinals/BRC-20 emerged on Bitcoin (Jan 2023).

## 2024 — ETF Cycle & Memecoin Season

- Bitcoin Spot ETF approved (Jan 2024): BTC broke $73K ATH (Mar 2024).
- Bitcoin halving (Apr 2024): block reward → 3.125 BTC.
- Solana memecoin season: BONK, WIF, POPCAT, massive pump.fi volume.
- Points programs replaced traditional airdrops as the primary farming meta.
- Hyperliquid launched HYPE (Nov 2024): largest airdrop in crypto history by value.
  No VC allocation. Fully community-distributed.
- EigenLayer restaking peaked: ~$20B TVL before token launch.

## 2025 — AI Agents & Perps Expansion

- AI agent tokens (Virtuals, ai16z) emerged as new narrative on Base/Solana.
- On-chain perps volume challenged CEX market share for the first time.
- Hyperliquid surpassed Binance perps in open interest on several days.
- Points-to-airdrop cycle commoditised: yield compressed on well-known farms.
- Institutional staking (ETH ETF staking) entered regulatory discussion.
- Total DeFi TVL recovered above $100B by mid-2025.
