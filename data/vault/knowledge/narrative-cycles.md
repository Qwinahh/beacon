# Narrative Cycles — How Crypto Themes Rotate

Reference for the bot when identifying which narrative phase we're in and what typically follows.
Narratives are the market's meta-game. Price follows narrative, then fundamentals catch up (or don't).

---

## Mega-Narratives By Cycle

### Cycle 1 (2013–2014): Digital Gold
- BTC as store of value / censorship-resistant money
- Silk Road proved real-world use (drug markets, avoiding state control)
- Mt. Gox collapse ended the cycle

### Cycle 2 (2017–2018): ICO / Tokenize Everything
- ETH as platform for unlimited token issuance
- "Blockchain will disrupt [every industry]"
- 95% of ICOs went to zero
- Narrative killed by SEC enforcement

### Cycle 3 (2020–2022): DeFi → NFTs → L1 Wars → Metaverse
- **DeFi**: Banks without banks. Liquidity mining bootstraps everything.
- **NFTs**: Digital ownership, creator economy, gaming.
- **L1 Wars**: SOL, AVAX, NEAR, FTM competing with ETH.
- **Metaverse**: Facebook rebrands to Meta. SAND, MANA pump. Reality: no one uses it.
- **Play-to-Earn**: Axie proves the model, then proves it's Ponzi.
- Luna/FTX collapse killed the cycle

### Cycle 4 (2023–present): Institutional + Convergence
- **Spot ETFs**: BTC and ETH get TradFi distribution
- **RWAs**: Tokenized T-bills, bonds, real estate
- **AI + Crypto**: Decentralized compute, AI agents, on-chain inference
- **Rollup Wars (L2)**: ETH L2s (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, zkSync) dominate DeFi
- **Memecoins as culture**: Not going away — evolved into serious liquidity events
- **Consumer crypto**: Farcaster, Lens, Friend.tech, POAP — social on-chain

## Narrative Rotation Within Bull Markets

Typical sequence (not always in order):
1. **BTC dominance phase** — BTC leads, alts lag. Happens early cycle and after macro shocks.
2. **ETH catches up** — Usually 2-6 months after BTC ATH, ETH outperforms.
3. **L1 alts season** — SOL, AVAX, etc. pump on ETH gas complaints or ecosystem growth.
4. **DeFi tokens** — Governance tokens get bid, liquidity returns to protocols.
5. **NFT/gaming season** — Culture tokens, gaming narratives.
6. **Memecoin supercycle** — Cycle exhaust. Retail FOMO buys pure speculation.
7. **BTC dominance recovers** — Alts dump, capital flows back to BTC.

## Current Active Narratives (as of mid-2025 onwards)

### RWA (Real-World Assets) 🏦
- **What**: Tokenizing off-chain assets (T-bills, bonds, real estate, credit) on-chain.
- **Why now**: Institutional demand for on-chain yield that doesn't require crypto risk.
- **Key players**: BlackRock BUIDL, Franklin Templeton BENJI, Ondo Finance, Maple Finance, Centrifuge.
- **Signal that it's over**: When yields normalize with TradFi, advantage disappears.

### AI Agents / DeAI 🤖
- **What**: Autonomous agents that own wallets, transact on-chain, run inference.
- **Key players**: Virtuals Protocol, ai16z (ElizaOS framework), Bittensor (TAO), Render (RNDR), io.net.
- **Why interesting**: Convergence of the two biggest secular trends of 2023-2025.
- **Risk**: Most AI tokens have no revenue and are pure narrative.

### Perps DEX / Hyperliquid 📈
- **What**: On-chain perpetuals with CEX-level performance.
- **Why**: Post-FTX trust collapse → users want self-custody of trading.
- **Key players**: Hyperliquid (dominant), dYdX v4, GMX, Vertex, Aevo.
- **Signal**: Hyperliquid's daily volume regularly exceeds all ETH L2 DEXs combined.

### Solana Ecosystem 🌅
- **Why**: Consumer UX, fast + cheap, Firedancer for reliability, Jupiter aggregator dominance.
- **Hot sectors**: Memecoins (Pump.fun), DePIN (Helium, Hivemapper), compressed NFTs.
- **Risk**: Still dependent on Solana network stability (has had outages before).

### Bitcoin L2 / BTC DeFi ₿
- **What**: Wrapping BTC functionality, bringing yield to idle BTC.
- **Key players**: Babylon (BTC staking), Stacks (sBTC), Lombard (LBTC), Merlin Chain.
- **Catalyst**: Institutional BTC holders want yield without bridging risk.

### DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure) 📡
- **What**: Tokens incentivize building real-world infrastructure (wireless, mapping, storage, compute).
- **Key players**: Helium (HNT), Hivemapper (HONEY), Filecoin (FIL), Akash (AKT), io.net.
- **Why compelling**: Revenue-generating networks with token incentives for supply-side growth.

## Airdrop Narrative Arc

The airdrop meta has evolved dramatically:

**Era 1 (2020)**: Retroactive surprise. Uniswap UNI — $1,200 per wallet, no warning. Pure gift.

**Era 2 (2021-2022)**: Liquidity farming. Bridge assets, use protocol, hope for airdrop. ENS, Optimism OP.

**Era 3 (2022-2023)**: Points systems emerge. Blur, Arbitrum. "Do X to earn points → points → airdrop." Explicit.

**Era 4 (2023-2024)**: Professional farming. Thousands of wallets, coordinated strategies, dedicated communities. zkSync, LayerZero, EigenLayer.

**Era 5 (2024-2025)**: Adversarial anti-farming. Protocols use Sybil detection, self-reporting, geographic restrictions, minimum thresholds, lockups, vesting. Kaito Yaps system (social contribution) as alternative.

**What this means**: Each era's farming strategy is partially obsolete for the next. Multi-wallet strategies increasingly filtered out.

## How to Spot a New Narrative Early

1. **VC allocation shift**: Watch what a16z, Paradigm, Multicoin, Pantera are funding.
2. **Builder concentration**: Where are the best developers spending time?
3. **Trading desk rotation**: Where is smart money moving capital?
4. **Protocol revenue**: Which protocols actually have users paying?
5. **Social signal**: CT engagement per post on a topic rising rapidly.
6. **GitHub commits**: Activity on repos in a sector accelerating.
7. **Tokenomics unlocks**: Large unlocks = sell pressure incoming, not buying signal.
