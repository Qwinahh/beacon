# Crypto Narrative Cycles

Reference for the writer — use these to identify where a current narrative sits in its cycle and whether it is early, crowded, or ending.

---

## The Narrative Lifecycle (4 stages)

**1. Discovery** — Only researchers and early farmers are paying attention. TVL is small, token doesn't exist or is very low cap. CT is quiet. Best entry point.

**2. Accumulation** — Points programs launch, CT starts posting. Farming begins. "Hidden gem" threads appear. TVL grows fast. Still early but crowding starting.

**3. Distribution** — TGE announced or rumoured. Airdrop criteria drop. Farming yield collapses as capital floods in. Valuation debates dominate CT. Late to be positioning.

**4. Resolution** — Token launches. Early farmers exit. Price dumps unless fundamentals hold. Smart money already moved to the next cycle. CT calls it a rug or genius depending on their entry.

## Historical Narrative Cycles

### DeFi Summer (2020)
- Discovery: Compound COMP launch Jun 2020
- Accumulation: Yield farming proliferates — Uniswap, Curve, Yearn
- Distribution: Oct 2020 — new protocols daily, yield collapses
- Resolution: Nov 2020 crash, most protocols ghost-chained by 2021

### NFT Summer (2021)
- Discovery: CryptoPunks/BAYC pre-mainstream, early 2021
- Accumulation: OpenSea volume 10x, Bored Apes viral
- Distribution: Aug 2021 peak — 10k PFP projects daily
- Resolution: 2022 — 99% of collections to near-zero

### L2 Points Era (2023-2024)
- Discovery: Arbitrum airdrop validates the L2 thesis Mar 2023
- Accumulation: Base, Blast, Scroll, ZKsync all launch points programs
- Distribution: ZKsync/Starknet TGE mid-2024, yield collapses
- Resolution: Most L2 tokens trade below TGE within 3 months

### Perps DEX Wars (2023-2024)
- Discovery: GMX on Arbitrum, real yield thesis
- Accumulation: dYdX v4, Hyperliquid, Vertex, ApeX
- Distribution: Hyperliquid dominates — 60%+ market share by 2025
- Resolution: Survivors are Hyperliquid and niche chains. Rest fading.

### AI Agents (2024-2025)
- Discovery: Terminal of Truths, early agent frameworks
- Accumulation: Virtuals protocol, ai16z, GOAT framework
- Distribution: Hundreds of agent tokens, points programs everywhere
- Resolution: Ongoing — watching for the filter round

## Signals That a Narrative Is Peaking

- CT threads about it outnumber threads about actual mechanics
- "How to farm X" tutorials from accounts with 200 followers
- Project announces $50M+ raise with no product
- Influencer threads promising 10-100x returns
- Farming yield collapses but new capital still entering

## Signals That a Narrative Is Early

- Only 2-3 CT accounts covering it
- TVL under $100M but growing >20% weekly
- No points program yet, but protocol clearly will need one
- DeFiLlama category page has fewer than 5 protocols
- Mechanism is genuinely novel, not a fork
