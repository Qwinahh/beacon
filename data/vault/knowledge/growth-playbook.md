# Growth Playbook — 0 to 10k in DeFi/Perps CT, Quality-First

*Constraint up front: NO follow-churn tactics. Mass follow/unfollow is an
explicit platform-manipulation trigger, this account has already been
through shadowban checks, and penalties accrue to a persistent account
reputation score ([[knowledge/x-algorithm-2026]]). Nothing below risks
the account.*

## The engagement ladder

Growth at <10k is borrowed reach converted into owned reach. The ladder:

1. **Rung 1 — replies on Tier 3 accounts (10k-100k protocol researchers/risk teams).** Highest author-engagement odds. An author-engaged reply carries the +75-class signal and exposes us to their audience. 3-5 quality reply drafts/day ([[knowledge/reply-strategy]]).
2. **Rung 2 — replies on Tier 1 analysts (@DefiIgnas-class).** Lower odds, bigger payoff. Only with genuinely additive data.
3. **Rung 3 — the timeline converts.** Profile visitors from replies find 3-5 recent posts that are specific and right. Posts are the portfolio; replies are the marketing.
4. **Rung 4 — quote-tweets of our own old calls when they resolve.** "flagged this in june" with the receipt. Converts archive into authority.
5. **Rung 5 — bigger accounts start engaging us.** Don't force it; one author-engagement from a 100k account does more than a month of posting.

## Getting noticed by bigger accounts (without being a reply guy)

- Be the live-number reply when their post has a stale figure — corrections with sources get remembered
- Reference their PRIOR work when relevant ("the unwind you flagged in May") — demonstrating memory is rare and registers hard
- Never @ them in our own posts hoping for pickup; repeated @mentions of the same handle is a suppression label
- Vary targets: same account max ~1 reply/day, rotate across the week
- Our wrong_take_correction format is the highest-probability "big account QTs us" play — accounts love citing corrections that flatter their own skepticism

## Posting cadence & thread launch timing

- 3-5 posts/day max, every one past the quality gate. Per-post engagement decays past ~7/day and the Author Diversity Scorer makes our own posts compete in-feed
- Windows (UTC): 07:00-10:00 EU+Asia, 13:00-16:00 US/EU overlap (best), 19:00-22:00 US prime. One post per window, never burst
- Threads: max 1/week, launched at the TOP of the 13:00-16:00 UTC window on a weekday — threads need runway, and reach drop-off on later tweets means the hook must catch the peak
- After a post overperforms (>3x usual impressions), post the follow-up within 2-6 hours — viral posts grant a temporary boost to subsequent posts

## Pinned post strategy

The pin is the conversion page for profile visitors arriving from replies.
Best pin: our single best resolved call — prediction + receipt + outcome
in one self-contained post. Update whenever a better one resolves.
Never pin: intro posts ("welcome to my account"), follow-bait, threads
older than ~6 weeks (stale pin = dead account smell).

## Bio optimization

- Formula: what we actually do, specifically. "perps microstructure + stablecoin yield mechanics. positions disclosed." beats any clever bio
- No "DeFi enthusiast", no "opinions my own", no rocket emojis, no follower-goal vanity
- The bio's job: a profile visitor from a good reply decides in 3 seconds whether we're a specialist. Specialists get followed; generalists get scrolled past

## Monthly follower targets (quality-first, realistic)

Premium account, 3-5 posts/day, reply ladder running. These are
calibration targets, not promises — quality compounds late:

| Month | Followers | What matters that month |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | 100-300 | Reply ladder + beat established. Zero bits, all substance |
| 3-4 | 300-800 | First author-engagements from Tier 1s; first correction post that travels |
| 5-6 | 800-2,000 | A resolved call worth pinning. Mid-stage voice begins ([[knowledge/personality-development]]) |
| 7-9 | 2,000-4,500 | Contrarian reputation phase; track-record posts |
| 10-12 | 4,500-10,000 | One or two outlier posts do half the work — earned, not engineered |

If a month doubles its target, do NOT raise posting volume — the curve
came from quality scarcity, and volume is how accounts give it back.

## What we never do (the integrity floor)

- Follow-churn, engagement pods, DM "support groups", paid RTs
- Giveaway/airdrop-bait follower farming (poisons the follower graph the algorithm clusters us by)
- Buying anything: followers, views, "growth services"
- Engagement-bait formats — Grox classifiers profile them and the followers they attract never convert to readers
- Posting through a drawdown in quality just to keep cadence. Posting nothing is always available
